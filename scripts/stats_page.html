What are Base Stats?

Determine a Pokemon's Final Stat Values

Base stats are base values of the six primary stats of a Pokemon before getting affected by their Stat Alignment and other stat-changing factors like effects from moves and abilities. These stats include HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense and Speed.

Due to a change in stats, all Pokemon in Champions have max IVs, regardless if they didn't have max IVs if they originated from a mainline game.

In Champions, you can modify a Pokemon's stats before battle by changing their 510 stat points or Effort Values (EVs) to their stats or changing their stat alignment. For each Pokemon, you have 510 EVs to allocate across your Pokemon's stats with a cap of 252 EVs per stat. Additionally, unlike in previous games, Individual Values, or IVs, are not present in Champions.
